# Xylem Competition Alignment

The supplied 2026 problem statement asks for solutions that prevent, detect,
monitor, remove or reduce pollutants and promote sustainable water-quality
management. NADI fits the detection/monitoring and management pathway by
optimising confirmatory sampling and response under uncertainty.

Xylem's official 2024 challenge page lists three judging dimensions: impact,
challenge fit and feasibility, and innovation. The NADI package addresses them
as follows:

| Dimension | Present evidence | Remaining weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | Clear utility decision and measurable testing KPI | No prospective utility impact yet |
| Fit/feasibility | Working end-to-end software and public hydraulic benchmark | Local data and calibration needed |
| Innovation | Adaptive uncertainty-to-action with certificate and abstention | No demonstrated performance superiority |
