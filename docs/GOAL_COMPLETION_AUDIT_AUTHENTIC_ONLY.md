# Goal completion audit under the authentic-only constraint

## Verified complete

- Authentic endpoint and 14,069-record source audit.
- Leakage-safe past-only longitudinal features.
- Chronological training, calibration, validation, and locked test partitions.
- Calibrated logistic, histogram-gradient, and XGBoost model families.
- Trivial classification baselines and imbalance-safe metrics.
- Authentic multi-site decision rounds with hidden outcomes revealed only after selection.
- Random, routine, historical-risk, single-model, ensemble, Bayesian, and SDAIF policy comparisons under an equal budget.
- Paired round bootstrap comparisons.
- Mixed-integer posterior and worst-case regret optimisation.
- Real-coordinate monitoring zones, model disagreement, and an authentic-only dashboard.
- Data hashes, reproduction scripts, automated tests, and an authentic-submission audit.
- Application copy, project-page copy, eight-slide presentation, and 3–4 minute video script.
- A fail-closed prospective-pilot data contract and dashboard/API preflight
  check. It rejects timestamp leakage, unequal policy universes, missing
  laboratory records for ranked sites, and incomplete endpoint definitions
  before any outcome comparison is calculated.

## Not complete and not claimed

1. **Authentic drinking-water hydraulic contamination experiment.** No available resource joins real pipe topology and operating state with time-matched E. coli outcomes. The project will not replace this with generated events.
2. **Observed confirmatory-test delays.** The authentic source lacks operational result-return timestamps. NADI evaluates test order and does not invent delays.
3. **Node-specific pipe uncertainty.** Real valve states, tank levels, demands, and verified pipe connectivity are unavailable for the biological records.
4. **Prospective impact.** Retrospective locked evaluation is complete. The
   prospective protocol and preflight validator are complete, but a monitoring
   partner must provide a future sampling programme to establish live impact.
5. **Independent expert review.** The software and proofs are internally checked, but an external statistician, operations-researcher, or water-quality specialist has not signed off.
6. **Recorded video, project-page publication, and personal eligibility paperwork.** These require the student/team account, names, consent forms, and public links.

## Consequence

The authentic-only Xylem package supports a monitoring-site prioritisation claim. It does not support pipeline localisation, delayed operational response, Delhi-NCR performance, or exposure reduction. Those original network-level requirements remain blocked by missing paired field evidence, not by unfinished software.

The final public-data search identified the U.S. EPA 2023–2024 authentic distribution-water workbook. Its 144 dated records contain no positive E. coli result and no pipe topology or operating state, so it cannot support classifier training or branch localisation. The result is recorded in `docs/EPA_DISTRIBUTION_WATER_DATA_AUDIT.md`.
