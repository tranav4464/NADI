## Judging alignment

The official scoring uses three criteria, each scored from 1 to 5:

1. **Impact:** emphasise the 114 versus 80 and 84 confirmed-result comparison under equal testing capacity.
2. **Challenge fit and feasibility:** demonstrate the working dashboard, authentic locked evidence, reproducible code, and specific prospective pilot pathway.
3. **Innovation:** explain the uncertainty-to-action mathematics, adaptive result sequence, and auditable regret objective. Do not rely on unsupported global novelty claims.

## Deadline caution

The official rules state that entries must be received by **1:00 PM Eastern
Time on 20 November 2026**. The event page displays a later 4:00 PM Eastern
Time equivalent. Submit before **1:00 PM Eastern Time**, then preserve a
screenshot or confirmation showing that the project page, video link and slide
link were published. Do not rely on the later displayed time.
