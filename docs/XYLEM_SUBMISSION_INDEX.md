## Do not use as Xylem validation evidence

The repository's older hydraulic, tracer, benchmark-network and generated-event
results are retained for reproducibility and future research. They are excluded
from the current Xylem metrics, figures and pitch.

## Exact demonstrated claim

NADI retrospectively prioritised authentic surface-water monitoring locations
using only information available before each sampling round and found more
laboratory-confirmed high-E.-coli observations within a three-test budget than
the declared random and routine-frequency comparators on the locked period.

## Exact boundary

The experiment does not validate a drinking-water pipe network, direct sensor
detection of E. coli, Delhi-NCR transfer, prospective deployment, a contaminated
branch, or avoided illness.
