# Phase 9 — scientific contribution and novelty audit

## Narrowest supported contribution

NADI's defensible contribution is an **executable, evidence-separated
decision-intelligence architecture for retrospective, fixed-budget
confirmatory-test prioritization**. It connects a calibrated mixed
environmental-water E. coli risk model with a transparent posterior/robust
testing rule, preserves uncertainty and abstention, and records the evidence
class and provenance of each supporting track.

The project does not establish a new general theorem, a direct E. coli sensor,
or a field-validated drinking-water contamination-response system.

## Prior-art relation

| Existing family | NADI uses | NADI does not claim |
|---|---|---|
| Environmental E. coli ML and weather models | chronological features, calibration, model comparisons | transfer from surface water to drinking-water pipes |
| Robust/minimax-regret decision theory | finite uncertainty vertices and regret optimisation | invention of minimax regret |
| Bayesian sequential testing | posterior update after selected result | superiority over adaptive Bayesian; authentic replay ties it |
| EPANET/WNTR source tracing | simulation-only movement and stress testing | biological validation from tracer reach |
| Operational anomaly detection | a separately labelled telemetry component | an anomaly is E. coli evidence |
| Data provenance/reproducible research practice | manifests, hashes, evidence labels and claim gating | publication novelty merely from integration |

The distinctive engineering contribution is the enforced chain from evidence
class through decision artifact, with explicit refusal to convert one evidence
type into another. This is useful and auditable, but not a claim of global
mathematical novelty.

## Strongest approved claims

1. On a frozen UK EA mixed environmental-water dataset, NADI retrospectively
   ranked observed same-round monitoring sites and found more high E. coli
   results within three tests than its declared routine and deterministic-random
   comparators.
2. The authentic decision evaluation ties the best single model and adaptive
   Bayesian comparator; it does not show SDAIF superiority.
3. NADI's authenticated workflow separates raw preparation, model training,
   calibration, locked evaluation, decision reconciliation, hydraulic
   reconstruction, simulation, reporting, and test execution with hashes and
   exclusive outputs.
4. NADI separately reconstructs measured pressure in BWFLnet and separately
   reruns Net3 simulation; these tracks remain explicitly non-biological.
5. Under site transfer and L-TOWN stress, performance weakens. The system's
   correct response is narrowed interpretation or abstention, not a universal
   performance claim.

## Claims excluded by evidence

The claim matrix and prohibited-claims file list language that must not appear
in the abstract, dashboard, figures, pitch, or manuscript as a demonstrated
result. These restrictions are part of the contribution: they prevent a
surface-water outcome, a pressure outcome, and a simulation outcome from being
presented as one field-confirmed pipeline event.

## Next decisive experiment

A prospective pilot would need pre-decision candidate sites, independently
selected control samples, collection and laboratory-release timestamps,
confirmed E. coli outcome/method/QC, bounded network mapping, and response
logs. Until such data exist, NADI remains a reproducible retrospective
test-prioritization system with separate supporting tracks.
