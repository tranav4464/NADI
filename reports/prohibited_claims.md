# Prohibited claims

Do not state or imply that NADI:

- directly detects E. coli from ordinary physicochemical sensors;
- detects faecal contamination in a live drinking-water pipe;
- localizes a contaminated drinking-water branch in a real city;
- is validated for Delhi, Ghaziabad, or another Indian municipal network;
- reduces illness, exposure, or mortality;
- integrates paired biological, hydraulic, sensor and network evidence from a
  single real contamination event;
- outperforms the strongest comparator on the authentic replay or Net3;
- transfers universally across cities or networks;
- has a globally novel theorem or guaranteed SCI-Q1 novelty;
- turns an EPANET/WNTR tracer, pressure estimate, telemetry anomaly, or risk
  score into confirmed microbial truth;
- treats the same-round retrospective reveal as real laboratory turnaround.
